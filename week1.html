MISSION 01

Virtual Safety

Safety Assessment Findings:

Safety Hazards Identified:

  • Electrical Safety Issues: Unsecured power cables and extension cords creating tripping hazards near equipment stations
  • PPE Compliance Gaps: Inconsistent use of safety glasses and hearing protection during machine operations
  • Workspace Organization: Tools and materials cluttered around equipment access points and emergency pathways

Exemplary Safety Practices Observed:

  • Emergency Preparedness: Clearly marked emergency stop buttons and well-documented emergency procedures
  • Comprehensive Signage: Detailed safety instructions and operating protocols displayed at each workstation
  • Systematic Maintenance: Regular equipment inspection logs and preventive maintenance schedules
