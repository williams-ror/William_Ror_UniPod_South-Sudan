Final Project: Portable Student Study Desk

CNC Fabrication Education Platform - Practical Implementation

Project Overview

I'm creating a CNC Fabrication Education Platform and will implement Portable Student Study Desks as a practical project. This initiative addresses the critical need for affordable, portable furniture for students in South Sudan while demonstrating comprehensive digital fabrication skills.

  • Primary Goal: Create foldable, portable desk for students without proper study furniture
  • Technical Focus: CNC machining, ergonomic design, mass production techniques
  • Educational Value:digital fabrication, and user-centered design
  • Local Impact: Solve practical problems using locally available materials and tools
 

Project Vision & Concept

This project combines digital fabrication education with practical impact by creating portable study desks that address real needs in South Sudanese education.

Dual Purpose Approach

Educational Platform: Serves as a comprehensive CNC fabrication learning resource with step-by-step tutorials, safety protocols, and project documentation.

Practical Implementation: Produces tangible, useful products that directly benefit students and demonstrate the value of digital fabrication skills.

Technical Foundation

The project will utilize my workshop's CNC equipment alongside other woodworking tools, creating a hybrid approach that's both technologically advanced and locally sustainable.

Problem & Solution

The Problem

  • Many students in South Sudan lack proper study surfaces at home
  • Traditional furniture is expensive and not portable
  • Limited resources for educational furniture in schools
  • Students often study on stools or uneven surfaces

Our Solution

  • Foldable wooden desks that students can use anywhere
  • Affordable production using local materials and CNC efficiency
  • Ergonomic design with book ledges and writing surfaces
  • Lightweight and portable for easy transportation

Fabrication Process

Materials Required:

  • Wood: 18mm MDF board & local timber
  • Hardware: bolts with nuts, screw nails
  • Finishing: Sandpaper, wood sealant and paint
  • Optional: Carry handles, non-slip pads

Step-by-Step Fabrication:

1. Digital Design Phase

  • Create CAD models in Fusion 360
  • Design interlocking parts for easy assembly
  • Generate CNC toolpaths for cutting and engraving
  • Test digital fit before fabrication

2. CNC Machining

  • Place MDF board on CNC bed
  • Execute cutting program for all components
  • Cut desk surface, legs, support braces
  • Engrave educational content or school or personal logos

3. Component Preparation

  • Sand all edges for student safety
  • Drill holes for bolt assembly
  • Test fit all components
  • Apply protective finish

4. Assembly & Testing

  • Assemble foldable legs using bolts
  • Attach book ledge and supports
  • Test folding mechanism and stability
  • Gather feedbacks for improvements
